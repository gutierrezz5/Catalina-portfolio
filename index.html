ABOUT

DESIGNING LEARNING EXPERIENCES WITH PURPOSE.

I’m an instructional designer passionate about creating digital learning experiences that feel thoughtful, engaging, and intuitive. My background in the U.S. Air Force and administrative work taught me to think about communication, organization, and how people use information in real-world environments. With a Master’s in Instructional Design and Technology from the University of Tampa, I bring together learning theory, technology, and creative design to build experiences that help people learn with purpose.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

My approach to instructional design centers on creating purposeful, engaging, and learner-centered digital experiences. I bring together learning theories, instructional strategies, and digital development to translate complex information into clear, accessible, and meaningful learning solutions.

This portfolio showcases my instructional design approach through selected planning documents, development artifacts, and interactive learning experiences. Each project demonstrates my commitment to thoughtful design, creative problem-solving, and learning experiences that support both learner needs and organizational objectives.
