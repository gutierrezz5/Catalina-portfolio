ABOUT

DESIGNING LEARNING EXPERIENCES WITH PURPOSE.

I’m an instructional designer passionate about creating digital learning experiences that feel thoughtful, engaging, and intuitive. My background in the U.S. Air Force and administrative work taught me to think about communication, organization, and how people use information in real-world environments. With a Master’s in Instructional Design and Technology from the University of Tampa, I bring together learning theory, technology, and creative design to build experiences that help people learn with purpose.
