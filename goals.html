01 / CAREER DIRECTION

MY PROFESSIONAL DIRECTION

My goal is to build a career creating engaging digital learning experiences for a range of organizations, including government and defense. I am interested in combining instructional design, technology, and creative development to make information more accessible and useful.

Over time, I hope to advance into project or program management, combining instructional design expertise with strategic planning, coordination, and leadership.

Through my graduate coursework and hands-on projects, I have developed a foundation in instructional design, digital development, visual communication, and project planning. I feel most confident in organizing information and designing learner-centered experiences, while continuing to strengthen my technical development, accessibility, and evaluation skills.
